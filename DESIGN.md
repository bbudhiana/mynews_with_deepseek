# Design System: Next Generation News Portal

## Thesis
A high-density, high-performance editorial newsroom. It refuses the generic blog layout in favor of a true media portal structure: immediate breaking news visibility, a dominant hero story, and a dense but scannable grid of latest and popular news. It feels urgent, credible, and fast.

## Own-World
- **Palette:** Deep space dark (`#0B1220`) as the primary background for reduced eye strain during long reading sessions. Editorial red (`#D71920`) used strictly for urgency (Breaking News, Live tags, primary CTAs). Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and slate grays (`#94A3B8`, `#CBD5E1`) for typography.
- **Typography:** `Inter` for UI elements, navigation, and metadata (dense, legible). `Plus Jakarta Sans` for headlines and article body (modern, geometric, authoritative).
- **Components:** Sharp edges (no rounded corners on news cards to maintain a serious editorial tone). Hairline borders (`#1E293B`) to separate dense content without visual noise.

## Story
The visitor arrives to understand the world right now. They immediately see the most critical breaking news, the day's top story, and can effortlessly scan categories or trending topics. The experience is frictionless, fast, and visually authoritative.

## First Viewport
- **Top:** Slim, sticky navigation bar with logo, category links, and search icon.
- **Below Nav:** A high-contrast, scrolling "Breaking News" ticker in editorial red.
- **Main Stage:** A massive Hero Story taking up 60% of the width (image, category badge, large headline, excerpt).
- **Sidebar (Right 40%):** "Popular News" list with large numbers (1, 2, 3) and compact headlines.
- **Below Fold:** A dense 3-column or 4-column grid of "Latest News" categorized by topic.

## Form
**Newsroom Modern** (Concept 1). Chosen for its direct alignment with the PRD's reference to CNN and Kompas.com, prioritizing information density and urgency over magazine-style whitespace.
